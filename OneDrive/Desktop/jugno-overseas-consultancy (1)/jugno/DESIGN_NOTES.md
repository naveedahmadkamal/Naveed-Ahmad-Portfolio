Design plan (brief pins down palette/type exactly, so following it precisely):

Color:
- Navy (bg-deep): #07152B
- Royal blue (bg-panel/section): #0B2343
- Gold (accent): #D8B45A
- White: #FFFFFF
- Light bg: #F7F8FB
- Derived: gold-dim #B8944A (hover), navy-line rgba(216,180,90,0.15) for hairlines, ink #12233F for body text on light bg

Type:
- Display: Playfair Display (headings, hero, section titles) — elegant, international consultancy feel
- Body/UI: Inter — clean, professional, highly legible at small sizes (forms, nav, cards)

Layout concept:
- Hero: full-bleed dark navy panel, faint great-circle flight-path line motif (SVG, gold, low opacity) connecting Islamabad to Dubai/Doha/Riyadh/KL — literal to "overseas documentation to specific destinations," not a stock travel photo. Two-column: heading/copy/buttons left, a stacked "document verification" card visual right (passport-style card with gold seal corner + stamp motif) — evokes attestation without any fake government emblem.
- Services: gold-numbered? No — services aren't a sequence, so use icon + label cards, not numbered.
- How It Works: IS a real sequence (4 steps) — numbered markers 01-04 are appropriate here per brief.
- Destinations: horizontal badge row / flag chips on light bg, understated.
- Signature element: the hero "document card" — a small stylised card with a gold wax-seal-style circular stamp and a subtle dotted verification line, echoed faintly again in the Apply/Contact section as a watermark. This is the one memorable, on-brand visual embellishment (ties to "attestation/verification" subject matter) — everything else stays restrained/corporate.

Motion: fade-in on scroll for sections, slide-up for cards (staggered), subtle hover lift on cards/buttons, gentle nav shadow on scroll. No parallax/gimmicks — corporate trust context calls for restraint.

Avoids AI-tell defaults: no cream/terracotta, no black+neon, no broadsheet hairline grid. Rounded corners (per brief: "rounded buttons," "elegant cards," "subtle shadows") — soft-corporate, not brutalist.
